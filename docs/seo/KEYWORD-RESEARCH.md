## Do these first

1. Give each service its own page (see the page plan below). The site is one page today, so it can only really rank for one topic.
2. Set the city. `CONFIG.location` in `index.html` is empty and no city appears in the text. Add “Alexandria, Egypt” (once confirmed) to the title, hero line, footer and structured data, and create a Google Business Profile.
3. Keep Google Search Console up to date: the site is verified there and `sitemap.xml` is submitted. Add every new page to the sitemap.

## What Google shows today

- Service searches in Alexandria and Cairo are led by marketplaces and directories (Twine, Upwork, Airbnb Services, Clutch, Wezoree, Dubizzle), not by photographers' own sites.
- Arabic results for photography services are mostly news articles, so the Arabic pages are the biggest opportunity.
- “Alexandria” alone pulls in Alexandria, Virginia and Greece. Always write “Alexandria, Egypt” in English copy.
- Food photography in Egypt is led by Cairo studios. In Alexandria the field is open, and the Charles American Food project is ready-made proof.
- “Reels creator” searches are mostly job ads; target “content for brands” phrasing instead.
